THE SCIENCE BEHIND THE SCENE

Watch, change, explain.

01 / FLIGHT

Forces work together

Wings help produce lift. Gravity pulls down. Air movement and wing motion can change how a bird travels. The flight lab simplifies these forces so you can compare their effects.

02 / FLOCKING

Small choices, big patterns

The flock models three simple local rules: avoid collisions, follow nearby headings, and stay near the group. Together they can create coordinated movement.

03 / THINK LIKE A SCIENTIST

Make a prediction

Choose one control to change. Predict what will happen, then watch the 3D models. Did your evidence support your idea?

These are stylised 3D bird models and simplified teaching simulations. Their numbers and motions illustrate relationships; they do not measure the flight of a real bird.
