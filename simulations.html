THE SCIENCE BEHIND THE SCENE

Watch, change, explain.

01 / GOLDEN EAGLE

Measured and modelled

One published flight model lists a 2.0 m wingspan and 16 m/s cruise speed for a golden eagle. A GPS flight study uses 0.75 m/s as a conservative best glide sink rate.

02 / RISING AIR

A simple balance

In this straight glide scenario, climb or sink speed equals rising air speed minus 0.75 m/s. A thermal faster than that can lift the bird without wingbeats.

03 / FLOCKING

Local decisions

The flock is a teaching model of three rules: keep space, match direction, and stay near neighbours. Its model units are not field measurements.

The speed and sink rate come from separate published studies and are combined here as a reference case. Real birds change speed, posture and route, and air currents vary. The 3D scene is stylised and its height is visually compressed.
